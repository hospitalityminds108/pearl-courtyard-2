PEARL COURTYARD — WEDDINGS & CELEBRATIONS
A Victorian heritage house in Godowlia, Varanasi

------------------------------------------------------------------
THE VENUES

1. THE ANTIQUE BANQUET
   A 150-guest hall beneath vintage chandeliers, carved timber and
   pearl-coated walls. Air-conditioned. Suited to a wedding
   reception, a sangeet, a mehndi lunch or a corporate evening.
   Seated: up to 150 guests. Floating: up to 180 guests.
   (Final capacity depends on layout and is confirmed on site visit.)

2. THE SHAMIYANA
   Our rooftop, open to the Varanasi sky. Best for 40-80 guests:
   a haldi brunch, a small ceremony, a family dinner, a proposal.
   Capacity and weather cover are confirmed with our events team.

3. SATVIK RESTAURANT
   A 35-cover, air-conditioned multi-cuisine restaurant for intimate
   family meals and rehearsal dinners.

------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE ARRANGE

  - Mandap and stage styling with our empanelled decorators
  - Banquet seating, buffet and live-counter layouts
  - Pure vegetarian and Jain menus, plus non-vegetarian on request
  - Welcome drinks, high tea and late-night chai
  - Guest accommodation across seven room categories (45 rooms)
  - Airport and railway station transfers
  - Boat rides on the Ganga for the couple and close family
  - Darshan assistance at Kashi Vishwanath Temple
  - Guest welcome kits and printed itineraries
  - Sound, lighting and projection

------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT WORKS

  1. Tell us the date, the guest count and the mood you have in mind.
  2. We hold the date provisionally for 48 hours, free of charge.
  3. A site visit - in person or on video - to walk the hall, the
     rooftop and the rooms.
  4. A written proposal: venue, menu, decor, rooms and a single
     consolidated quote. No hidden line items.
  5. A booking advance confirms the date; the balance is settled
     before the event per the agreed schedule.
