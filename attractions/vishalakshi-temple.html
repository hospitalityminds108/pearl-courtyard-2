Vishalakshi Temple

A Shakti shrine tucked above the ghats, for the unhurried kind of pilgrim.

The Short Version

Vishalakshi Temple, in plain words

Vishalakshi, the goddess with the wide, watchful eyes, is one of the most revered Shakti sites of Kashi. It sits in the lanes above the ghats, a short ride or a good morning walk from the hotel.

It is quieter than the big-name temples, which is exactly why we like sending guests here. The lanes leading to it are half the experience: bells, flower sellers, tea stalls and a few cows who clearly know the way.

Navratri is the time to be here if you enjoy a crowd with a glow. At other times, early morning is peaceful.

Visitor Tips

Before you go

  • Wear shoes you can slip off easily.
  • Ask our desk for the easiest lane. Google Maps sometimes picks the bumpy one.
  • Pair it with a sunrise boat ride from Dashashwamedh.
  • Keep the offering simple. Flowers and a smile work wonders.
