Weddings & Celebrations

Weddings in Kashi

Where things begin. Under carved timber and vintage chandeliers, or on an open rooftop above the old city — we host the celebrations families remember for decades.

The Venue

A wedding in the
oldest living city.

Varanasi is not a backdrop. It is a blessing. Families come here to marry because the city itself is considered auspicious — and because a wedding here carries a weight of meaning that no banquet hall in a suburb can offer.

Pearl Courtyard hosts the whole arc of it: the mehendi in the afternoon, the haldi in the courtyard, the sangeet under the chandeliers, the reception on the rooftop with the city lights below.

We are a boutique house, not a convention hotel. That means one wedding at a time, a team that knows your family by name by the second day, and a kitchen that cooks for your guests rather than for a volume target.
