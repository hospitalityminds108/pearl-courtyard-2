150Years of the house
Welcome to Pearl Courtyard

A charitable house,
reborn in pearl and teak.

The building began as a 150-year-old charitable house run by a Bengali Trust, welcoming travellers from across India. Its present owners rebuilt it as a Victorian masterpiece — the exterior largely unchanged, the interiors reimagined for modern comfort.

Pearl Courtyard is designed in teak and finished in pearl, in Varanasi's most pious quarter — the Kashi Vishwanath Dham. Fifty-four keys across seven categories, antique-style furniture, and the quiet confidence of a house that knows how to look after people.
