The Room

Triple Bed Room

Three proper beds, 350 square feet, and large windows that make the room feel considerably bigger than its footprint. The Triple Bed Room was designed for the group that travels together but does not want to negotiate over who shares.

The antique-style furniture gives it the same warmth as the rest of the house, while the layout keeps a clear path between beds, bathroom and door — practical in a way that matters when three people are unpacking at once.

It is a favourite of friends on a pilgrimage, siblings travelling with a parent, and small families who want everyone in one room without compromise.
