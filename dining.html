Pure Veg Dining in Godowlia

Satvik — Pure Veg

A 35-cover, air-conditioned pure veg restaurant near Kashi Vishwanath — warm timber, quiet service and Jain-friendly cooking from the morning market.

The Restaurant

A pure veg, multi-cuisine
table for every mood.

Satvik is the restaurant of our hotel near Kashi Vishwanath Temple: 35 covers, air-conditioned, with service that makes a guest feel special without fuss.

The kitchen is pure veg and multi-cuisine, because our guests arrive from everywhere — a family from Kanpur, a couple from Kyoto, a consultant from Mumbai who wants a light paneer tikka at nine. Jain-friendly food, without onion or garlic, is made on request.

Breakfast begins early for a 6 a.m. darshan. Lunch is a refuge from the heat. Dinner runs late, after the Ganga Aarti.
