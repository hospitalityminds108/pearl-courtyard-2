Vindhyachal Vindhyavasini Temple

The Mother who lives in the hills, a full-hearted day trip from Kashi.

The Short Version

Vindhyachal Vindhyavasini Temple, in plain words

Vindhyachal is the sacred seat of Goddess Vindhyavasini, on the bank of the Ganga in Mirzapur district. It is a very doable day trip from Varanasi, roughly 65 km and 1.5–2 hours by road, depending on traffic.

The classic circuit is the Trikon parikrama: Vindhyavasini, Ashtabhuja on its hillock, and Kali Khoh. Navratri brings huge crowds in both Chaitra (spring) and Ashwin (autumn), so if you want the glow without the squeeze, go on a weekday outside festival days.

Leave early, carry water, and let our travel desk arrange a car and a driver who knows the shortcuts. You will be back in time for dinner at Satvik.

Visitor Tips

Before you go

  • Start by 6 am to beat traffic and the crowds.
  • Allow a full day if you plan the three-temple circuit.
  • Navratri is magical but extremely busy. Book your car in advance.
  • Distances and timings vary. Our desk confirms the route the evening before.
